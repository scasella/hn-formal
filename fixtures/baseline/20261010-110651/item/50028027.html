Prion diseases include:

-in sheep, scrapie

-in humans, Kuru and Creutzfeldt-Jacob

-in cows, BSE ("mad cow")

-in deer, Chronic Wasting Disease (CWD)

-in cats, feline spongiform encephalopathy

Scrapie is particularly known, deadly, and long-lived. The toxin proteins can survive for a decade in soil, and infect a new host years after it emerged.

shymaple 3 hours ago | prev [–]

What Sonia Vallabh and Eric Minikel have been working toward since her diagnosis in 2011 is truly inspiring. The drug candidate was developed by Anastasia Khvorova and her lab at UMass Chan Medical School, in collaboration with Vallabh, Minikel, and their research teams.

What makes this effort even more remarkable is their commitment to sharing the drug development process openly, including an IND filing that would typically remain confidential.

Whatever the eventual outcome( I’m optimistic) about the possibilities, the journey, the data, and the knowledge shared along the way will be valuable.

This is not just about developing a drug; it’s about making science more transparent, collaborative, and accessible for everyone working toward the same goal.
